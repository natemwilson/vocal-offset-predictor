How It Works

  • Input: A subtitle text and its total duration
  • Model: Predicts how long each word takes to speak based on word length, syllables, and context
  • Output: Start and end times for each word, enabling word-by-word display
  • Use Case: Display subtitles that follow along with speech instead of showing everything at once
